Our Legacy

Pioneering safe, scientific, and sustainable poultry solutions since 2016

Rooted in Tradition, Powered by Innovation

Welcome to S K Groups, a premier poultry supplier and agricultural tech partner based in Avaragolla, Davanagere, Karnataka. We specialize in producing robust, highly-productive BV380 and BV300 laying hens, heavy-duty hot-dip galvanized cages, scientifically-formulated feeds, and modern farming equipment.

Our operational model is built around strict biological security protocols and cutting-edge flock husbandry. We work tirelessly to ensure that every chick hatched, cage structured, and sack of feed processed meets elite quality standards.

Certified Safety

100% pathologically screened, vaccinated birds and lab-certified feed blends.

Trusted Scale

Proudly partnering with over 5,000 commercial and small-scale farmers nationwide.

Our Mission

Empowering Farmers with Quality & Science

To supply the highest-grade poultry livestock, grain feed, and housing equipment direct from our bio-secure farms. We strive to support growers with integrated veterinary insights, ensuring sustainable productivity, optimal flock health, and maximum profitability.

Our Vision

The Benchmark of Bio-Secure Farming

To become India's most trusted, fully integrated agriculture-tech partner. We envision a future where even the smallest poultry farmer can access cutting-edge bio-safety protocols, advanced automation equipment, and premier grain nutrition at fair wholesale prices.

Our Milestones

The journey of S K Groups from inception to one of Karnataka's leading poultry suppliers

2016

The Inception

Started S K Groups in Davanagere with a small flock of layer hens and a vision to supply clean, high-yielding breeds to regional poultry farmers.

2019

Cages & Equipment Launch

Expanded operations to include premium hot-dip galvanized tier cages and automated nipple drinkers, helping farmers reduce feed waste and labor.

2022

Scientific Feed Formulations

Established a state-of-the-art feed blending unit, supplying starter, grower, layer ready feeds and high-protein concentrate mixes.

2026

Digital Expansion & Innovation

Reaching over 5,000 satisfied farmers, offering integrated veterinary consultations, bio-safe logistics, and comprehensive farm setup services.
