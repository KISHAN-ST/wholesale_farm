Our Story

A legacy of quality poultry farming since 2016

Rooted in Tradition, Powered by Innovation

Welcome to S K Groups. We are a trusted supplier based in Avaragolla, Davanagere. We specialize in BV380 and BV300 Birds, Cages, Feed and also all kinds of poultry services.

Our journey started with a simple belief: healthy birds produce the best results. That's why we maintain strict bio-security measures and employ modern farming techniques to ensure that every hen, cage, and sack of feed that leaves our farm meets the highest standards of quality.

Whether you are a small-scale farmer looking to start your journey or a large enterprise needing bulk supplies, we are here to support your growth with direct-from-farm pricing and expert guidance.
