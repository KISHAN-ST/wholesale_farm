Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common poultry farm management and order inquiries

What is the difference between BV380 and BV300 laying hens?

BV380 brown layers are widely known for producing dark brown eggs and have excellent resilience in warm climates. BV300 layers are highly productive white-egg laying birds focused on high feed conversion efficiency and consistent shell density. Both are fully vaccinated at our farm before sale.

How do automatic nipple drinkers reduce water wastage?

Nipple drinkers release water only when pecked by the bird. This prevents open pools of water from being contaminated by dirt or tipped over onto the litter, maintaining dry coop conditions and reducing disease spread.

Can you customize cage arrays for specific barn sizes?

Yes, S K Groups offers specialized consulting and installation. We design and structure GI layer cage arrays in 3-tier and 4-tier configurations based on your barn's dimensions and ventilation guidelines.

Do you provide custom feed mixtures?

We supply a wide range of standard pre-mixed starter, grower, and layer rations. For farmers who have access to local grains (like maize), we provide professional 30% or 35% feed Concentrate bags along with specific mixing formula cards to optimize costs.
